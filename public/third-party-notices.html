Additional offline trees

Mammals: Upham NS, Esselstyn JA & Jetz W (2019), Inferring the mammal tree: Species-level sets of phylogenies for questions in ecology, evolution, and conservation. Paper; Dryad version 4, S3. The global ML tree contains 4,098 mammal species and one lizard outgroup. Exact Newick bytes unchanged.

Primates: dos Reis M, Gunnell GF, Barba-Montoya J, Wilkins A, Yang Z & Yoder AD (2018), Using Phylogenomic Data to Explore the Effects of Relaxed Clocks and Calibration Strategies on Divergence Time Estimation: Primates as a Test Case. Paper; Dryad version 1. Selected entry: timetrees/372s-sA-AR-fig.tree, 367 primates plus five outgroups. Only its NEXUS envelope is removed; original Newick tokens and comments are retained. The dated source is used as a nonmetric topology.

Both selected Dryad data components are explicitly CC0-1.0. Their scientific citations are retained for provenance; no new attribution requirement is imposed by this software.

Plants: Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew and collaborators, Kew Tree of Life Explorer release 4.0 (April 2026). Baker WJ et al. (2022), A Comprehensive Phylogenomic Platform for Exploring the Angiosperm Tree of Life. Systematic Biology 71:301–319. DOI. The species, family and order tree files are included unchanged under CC BY 4.0. Retain credit, release, source/license links and modification notices, and apply no additional restrictions to the licensed data. No endorsement is implied. These files include gymnosperm outgroups; species-level tips are samples, not necessarily unique species.

Opening a dataset creates explicit collapsed overview groups without dropping original tips; subsequent selection, reduction and drawing changes are recorded in projects and exports. Acquisition and extraction audit records exact hashes, entry paths, counts and reuse evidence. Complete source archives and their other contents are excluded.
