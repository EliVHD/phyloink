    Classification ranks

    This editable order is a drawing policy, not detected source data. Confirm it against your framework. Absent ranks stay absent.

    Use exact source IDs and policy names. Null means explicitly unranked; omit an ID for unknown rank. You can also select a junction and assign its rank below.     Source distance scale
